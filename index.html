Running this in the field

Phone GPS, the microphone, and weather lookups only work when the page is served from an https:// address and opened as its own page — not inside an app's preview frame. Put the file on GitHub Pages, Netlify Drop, or your office server, open it in Safari or Chrome, then add it to your home screen. Weather comes from Open-Meteo, address lookups from OpenStreetMap.
